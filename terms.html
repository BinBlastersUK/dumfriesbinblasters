Window Cleaning

Customers should ensure reasonable access to the windows being cleaned. Windows should be closed before exterior cleaning takes place.

Where possible, items that prevent safe access to windows should be moved before the service. Dumfries Bin Blasters may decide not to clean a window or area where access is unsafe or impractical.

Window cleaning results can be affected by the condition of the glass, frames and surrounding surfaces. Permanent staining, scratches, damaged seals, failed double-glazed units, paint, cement, silicone, mineral deposits or other ingrained marks may not be removable through standard window cleaning.

Gutter Clearing

Gutter clearing is carried out using appropriate professional equipment and, where suitable, a gutter vacuum system operated from ground level.

The service is intended to remove accessible loose debris such as leaves, moss and general build-up from guttering.

Dumfries Bin Blasters may be unable to clear sections of guttering where access is unsafe, the guttering is damaged, blocked internally, obstructed by fixed structures or where completing the work could cause damage.

Gutter clearing does not include repair or replacement of damaged gutters, downpipes, roof components or drainage systems unless specifically agreed.

Fascia & Soffit Cleaning

Fascia and soffit cleaning is intended to remove general dirt, organic build-up and surface grime.

Some staining, weathering, oxidation, discolouration, paint damage or ingrained marks may remain after cleaning and may not be removable through normal exterior cleaning.

Cleaning Results

Dumfries Bin Blasters aims to complete all services to a high standard. However, cleaning cannot guarantee the removal of every stain, mark or contaminant.

Results can depend on the age, condition and material of the surface being cleaned. Permanent staining, discolouration, corrosion, damage, paint, rust, residue, scratches, weathering or ingrained marks may not be removable.
