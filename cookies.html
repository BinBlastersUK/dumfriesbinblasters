This Cookie Policy explains how cookies may be used on the Dumfries Bin Blasters website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites function correctly, remember settings, improve security and provide information about how the website is used.

How this website may use cookies

Cookies may be used for:

  • Basic website functionality
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Remembering preferences
  • Understanding website performance
  • Supporting booking or contact features

Essential cookies

Essential cookies are required for parts of a website to operate correctly. These may be used without consent where they are strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the visitor.

Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies may be used to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed and whether any technical problems occur.

Analytics cookies should only be used where the required consent has been obtained.

Third-party services

The website may link to or use third-party services, including booking, social-media, mapping, video, review or payment services.

These providers may set their own cookies when you use their services or visit their websites. Their use of cookies is governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of a website function.

Current website use

The main Dumfries Bin Blasters website is a simple informational website. Some third-party services linked from the site, including the booking system and social-media websites, may use their own cookies.

Changes to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if the website or the services used by Dumfries Bin Blasters change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
