Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the Dumfries Bin Blasters website and when accessing services linked from the website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

Cookies can help websites operate correctly, remember settings, improve security and provide information about how a website is used.

How Our Website May Use Cookies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for purposes including:

  • Basic website functionality
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Remembering settings or preferences
  • Understanding website performance
  • Supporting booking and quote features
  • Supporting services provided by third-party platforms

Essential Cookies

Some cookies are necessary for a website or online service to operate correctly.

Strictly necessary cookies may be used where required to provide a service requested by the visitor, such as security, session management or essential booking functionality.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies may be used to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors reach the site and whether technical problems occur.

Where analytics cookies require consent, they should only be activated after the appropriate consent has been obtained.

Booking Hub

Dumfries Bin Blasters provides links to an online Booking Hub where customers can book wheelie-bin cleaning or request quotes for exterior cleaning services.

The Booking Hub may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for its operation, security, form processing or other functionality.

Cookies used by services supporting the Booking Hub may be governed by the policies of the relevant technology providers.

Third-Party Services

Our website contains links to or may interact with third-party services, including:

  • Online booking and quote services
  • Google services
  • Social-media platforms
  • WhatsApp
  • Email services
  • Review services
  • Website hosting and technical providers

If you follow a link to a third-party website or service, that provider may set its own cookies or collect information according to its own privacy and cookie policies.

Dumfries Bin Blasters does not control cookies placed directly by third-party websites after you leave our website.

External Links

The Dumfries Bin Blasters website includes links to external services such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Google reviews and the Booking Hub.

Simply displaying a link does not necessarily mean that the third-party service places cookies on your device while you remain on the Dumfries Bin Blasters website.

However, once you open an external service, that provider may use cookies according to its own policies.

Your Choices

Most web browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their privacy or browser settings.

You may also be able to prevent certain categories of cookies from being stored.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent some website, booking or online-service features from working correctly.

Current Website Use

The main Dumfries Bin Blasters website is primarily an informational exterior-cleaning website containing service information, pricing, customer reviews, before-and-after images and links to the Booking Hub and external communication platforms.

The website currently provides information about wheelie-bin cleaning, window cleaning, gutter clearing and fascia and soffit cleaning.

Services accessed through external links or the Booking Hub may use their own cookies or similar technologies.
