FAQ

What services do you offer?

Dumfries Bin Blasters offers wheelie bin cleaning, window cleaning, gutter clearing and fascia & soffit cleaning for residential and commercial customers.

Do you clean inside and outside wheelie bins?

Yes. Wheelie-bin cleaning includes an internal and external wash and sanitising treatment.

Do I need to leave my bin out?

Yes. Please leave your bin accessible after it has been emptied and we'll take care of the rest.

How do window cleaning quotes work?

Window cleaning is available for residential and commercial properties. Prices depend on the property and the number of windows to be cleaned. Submit a no-obligation quote request through the Booking Hub and I'll confirm the price.

How are gutters cleared?

Residential and commercial gutter clearing is carried out using a professional gutter vacuum system, allowing gutters to be cleared from ground level where suitable.

Do you clean fascias and soffits?

Yes. Residential and commercial fascia and soffit cleaning is available and is quoted based on the property and the amount of cleaning required.

When do I pay?

Payment is due on the day of your clean by bank transfer or cash, unless agreed otherwise.
