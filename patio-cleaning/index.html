Patio Cleaning FAQs

How much does patio cleaning cost?

Prices depend on the size, surface type, access and condition of the patio. Submit a quote request and I'll confirm the price after assessing the work required.

Do you offer one-off patio cleaning?

Yes. Patio cleaning is available as a one-off service, with no ongoing cleaning plan required.

Can you clean paths as well as patios?

Yes, suitable paved paths and other outdoor hard surfaces can be included in a quote.

Do you use a pressure washer?

Pressure washing may be used where appropriate. The cleaning method depends on the surface material and its condition.

Will patio cleaning remove every stain?

Results vary depending on the surface and the type and age of staining. Some marks may remain, and we'll discuss any limitations before work begins.
