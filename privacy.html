Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Dumfries Bin Blasters respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when contacting us, requesting a quote or booking our services.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your address and postcode
  • Your telephone number
  • Your email address
  • Your selected service and bin-cleaning requirements
  • Booking, payment and customer communication records
  • Any other information you provide through our booking form

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage your booking
  • Provide wheelie-bin cleaning services
  • Contact you regarding your booking or service
  • Arrange recurring cleans
  • Send invoices and maintain business records
  • Respond to questions, requests or complaints
  • Meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations

Our lawful basis for using your information

We normally process your information because it is necessary to provide the service you requested, to take steps before providing that service, to comply with legal obligations or for legitimate business purposes such as maintaining customer and accounting records.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only where necessary with trusted service providers used to operate the business, such as booking, customer-management, payment, email, website-hosting and accounting services.

Information may also be shared where required by law, HM Revenue & Customs or another lawful authority.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage customer accounts and comply with legal, tax and accounting requirements.

How we protect your information

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for incorrect information to be corrected, request deletion or restriction, or object to certain uses of your information.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.

Changes to this policy
