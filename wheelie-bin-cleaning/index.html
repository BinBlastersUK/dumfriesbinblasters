Wheelie Bin Cleaning FAQs

Do you clean inside and outside the bin?

Yes. Wheelie-bin cleaning includes an internal and external pressure wash and sanitising treatment.

When is my bin cleaned?

Bins are normally cleaned after your council collection. Please leave the empty bin accessible after collection.

Do you clean commercial bins?

Yes. Commercial and large-bin cleaning is available, with pricing based on bin size, quantity and frequency.
